## Task 4 – Data Storytelling & Statistical Validation

In Task 4, I converted the analytical findings into a clear business story and statistically validated an important finding.

### Statistical Test

**Business Question:**  
Is there a significant relationship between Age and Total Sales?

### Hypotheses

**H0:** There is no significant relationship between Age and Total Sales.

**H1:** There is a significant relationship between Age and Total Sales.

### Test Used

Pearson Correlation Significance Test

### Results

- Sample Size: 1,000
- Correlation (r): 0.001294
- P-value: 0.967395
- Significance Level: 0.05

Since the p-value is greater than 0.05, the null hypothesis was not rejected.

The analysis did not find a statistically significant linear relationship between Age and Total Sales.

### Business Recommendations

- Focus on high-performing categories such as Electronics.
- Strengthen top-performing products such as Laptop.
- Expand opportunities in high-performing city markets such as Patna.
- Use price and quantity insights for promotions and demand planning.
- Avoid relying on age alone for sales targeting.
